Programs

Three programs. One goal: a fully funded place.

Pick the one you're aiming for. Many students prepare for more than one.

FLEX

Future Leaders Exchange

A year at an American high school, living with a host family, fully funded by the U.S. Department of State.

For: school students in Uzbekistan, usually aged 15 to 17.

Mentoring: free

How we help

  • Preparation for each selection round
  • Essay drafts reviewed line by line
  • Mock interviews with FLEX alumni
  • Application form checks before you submit

UWC

United World Colleges

Two years at a UWC school abroad, earning the IB Diploma, with scholarships based on need.

For: secondary school students, roughly 16 to 18.

Mentoring: free

How we help

  • Personal essays and short answers
  • Describing your activities and achievements
  • Selection day and interview practice
  • A final review before you submit

YYGS

Yale Young Global Scholars

An intensive summer program at Yale University, with generous need-based financial aid.

For: high school students in grade 10 or 11.

Mentoring: free

How we help

  • Essays and short answers
  • Your activities list and achievements
  • Guidance on the financial aid application
  • A final review before you submit

FAQ

Questions people ask us

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee, no deposit and no paid tier later. We started Scholar's Hub because good feedback shouldn't depend on your family's budget.

Who can apply?

Students in Uzbekistan who are preparing for FLEX, UWC or YYGS. We give priority to motivated students with strong potential and limited funding.

How do you choose students?

We read every application. Our mentors' time is limited, so we can't take everyone, but we try to reply to every applicant on Telegram.

Do you guarantee admission?

No one honestly can. The programs make the final decision. What we promise is careful, honest feedback that makes your application as strong as it can be.

Where do sessions happen?

Online, mainly through Telegram, so you can join from any region of Uzbekistan.

How do I join the webinars?

Join our Telegram channel @scholars_hubprograms. Every webinar is announced there with the date and link.

Can I prepare for more than one program?

Yes. Many essay and interview skills overlap, and we can help you plan for more than one.
